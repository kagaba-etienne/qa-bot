Article 1: Imposition of the penalty of
community service
Article 35 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
(1) « In case the penalty of community
service is imposed as principal penalty,
the court sets the time limit for serving
such a penalty. Such a time limit cannot
exceed 6 months.
(2) The penalty of community service may
be imposed in lieu of another principal
penalty. In that case, it is imposed as
follows:
(a) when an offence is punishable by
imprisonment for a term of more
than 6 months and not more than 5
years, the court may order that the
convict serve part of his or her
penalty by performing community
service for a period not exceeding 2


years;
(b) when an offence is punishable by
imprisonment for a term of less
than 6 months, the court may order
that the convict serve the penalty of
community service for a period not
exceeding the maximum
imprisonment provided by the law
for such an offence.
(3) In case of the convict’s wilful failure to
properly serve the penalty of
community service, he or she serves the
remainder of the penalty in a
correctional facility. »
Article 2: Reduction of penalty in case of
mitigating circumstances decided by a judge
Article 60 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
« If the judge approves mitigating
circumstances, he or she may reduce penalties
as follows:


(a) life imprisonment may be reduced to a
penalty of imprisonment of not less
than 15 years;
(b) a fixed-term imprisonment from 6
months and above may be reduced but
it cannot be less than a half of the
minimum sentence provided for the
offence committed;
(c) a penalty of imprisonment of less than
6 months may be suspended;
(d) a fine may be reduced up to a quarter of
the minimum fine provided for the
offence committed;
(e) a penalty of community service may be
suspended. »
Article 3: Conditions for application of
suspension of penalty
Article 64 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
(1) « If the convicted person was not
previously convicted of another penalty
of imprisonment exceeding 6 months,


the court may, through a reasoned
decision, order suspension of the
execution of all or part of the principal
or accessory penalty imposed provided
that the principal penalty imposed does
not exceed 5 years.
(2) The court decision ordering suspension
of penalty –
(a) states the grounds on which it is
based;
(b) states when the suspension of the
penalty of imprisonment or the
penalty of community service
begins and when it ends;
(c) may order suspension of all or part
of the penalty of imprisonment or
of fine;
(d) may order suspension of the
principal or accessory penalty.
(3) The court decision ordering suspension


of penalty is rendered during the trial
for the offence committed. »
Article 4: Punishment of a co-offender and
an accomplice
Article 84 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
(1) « The co-offender or accomplice is
punished according to such penalties
for the offender as are provided for by
the law.
(2) The co-offender or accomplice is
prosecuted even if the criminal action
cannot be instituted against the
offender due to the reasons specific to
the offender such as death, insanity or
his or her being unidentified. However,
when the person referred to in items 5
d), 5 e) and 5 f) of Article 2 of this Law
is the offender’s spouse or relative up
to the second degree, he or she is
exempted from prosecution for petty
offences and misdemeanours. »


Article 5: Punishment of the crime of
genocide
Article 92 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
« A person who commits any of the acts
referred to in Article 91 of this Law commits
an offence. Upon conviction, the person is
liable to the penalty of life imprisonment. »
Article 6: Other acts characterised as war
crimes
Article 99 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
« Any of the following acts committed in
armed conflicts is a war crime:
(a) to employ poisonous weapons or other
weapons calculated to cause
unnecessary suffering;
(b) to attack medical units and
establishments as well as to destruct
and damage medical transport facilities
and aircrafts;


(c) to use asphyxiating, poisonous or other
similar gases, or bacteriological
methods of warfare;
(d) to produce, stockpile, acquire or
transport biological products or toxins,
whatever their origin or method of
production, for hostile purposes in
armed conflict;
(e) to outrage human dignity, particularly
through rape, sexual torture, forced
prostitution and any form of indecent
assault;
(f) to enslave and engage in slave trade, or
commit slavery-like practices and
forced labour in any form;
(g) to use human shields;


(h) to starve the civilian population and
prevent humanitarian assistance from
reaching such population;
(i) to deliberately separate children from
their parents or other persons
responsible for their safety and welfare;
(j) to force civilians, including children to
take part in hostilities or to perform
works related to military service
purposes;
(k) to refuse to cater for the wounded, sick,
shipwrecked persons and persons
whose liberty has been restricted for
reasons related to the armed conflict;
(l) to mistreat prisoners or internees;
(m) to loot or destroy public or private
property;
(n) to impose collective punishment for
individual acts;
(o) to commit acts of violence meant to
terrorise all or part of the population. »


Article 7: Penalties for other acts
characterised as war crimes
Article 100 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
« A person convicted of any of the offences
referred to under Article 99 of this Law is liable
to –
(a) life imprisonment if he or she has
committed any of the acts referred to
under Subparagraphs (a), (b), (c), (d),
(e), (f), (g), (h) and (i);
(b) imprisonment for a term not less than
10 years but not more than 15 years, if
he or she has committed any of the acts
referred to under Subparagraphs (j), (k)
and (l);
(c) imprisonment for a term not less than 5
years but not more than 10 years, if he
or she has committed any of the acts
referred to under Subparagraphs (m),
(n) and (o). »
Article 8: Infanticide
Article 108 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general


is amended as follows:
« Article 108: Infanticide
A person who kills his or her biological child
whose age is not above 12 months commits
infanticide. Upon conviction, he or she is liable
to life imprisonment. However, if a woman
convicted of infanticide has committed it due
to postpartum depression or by effect of
lactation certified by an authorised medical
doctor or an authorised psychiatrist, he or she
is liable to imprisonment for a term of not less
than 6 months but not more than 2 years. »
Article 9: Transmission of a disease to
another person
Article 117 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
« A person who wilfully transmits a disease to
another person commits an offence. Upon
conviction, he or she is liable to imprisonment
for a term of not less than 2 years but not more
than 3 years and a fine of not less than FRW
300,000 but not more than FRW 500,000. If the
transmitted disease is incurable or leaves the
person permanently disabled, the penalty is


imprisonment of not less than 20 years but not
more than 25 years and a fine of not less than
FRW 500,000 but not more than FRW
1,000,000.»
Article 10: Unintentional assault or battery
Article 120 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
(1) « A person who, because of clumsiness,
recklessness, negligence, carelessness,
lack of precaution and foresight,
assaults or batters another person,
commits an offence. Upon conviction,
he or she is liable to imprisonment for
a term of not less than 8 days but not
more than 2 months and a fine of not
less than FRW 100,000 but not more
than FRW 200,000 or one of these
penalties.
(2) If the offender causes injuries to several
persons, he or she is liable to
imprisonment for a term of not less than
2 months but not more than 3 months
and a fine of not less than FRW 500,000
but not more than FRW 1,000,000 or


one of these penalties.»
Article 11: Intentional assault or battery
Article 121 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
(1) « A person who, wilfully, injures, beats
or commits any serious violence
against another person, commits an
offence. Upon conviction, he or she is
liable to imprisonment for a term of not
less than 6 months but not more than 1
year and a fine of not less than FRW
100,000 but not more than FRW
300,000.
(2) If the offence referred to in this Article
is committed against a child, a parent, a
spouse or a person unable to protect
himself or herself due to his or her
physical or mental state, the offender is
liable to imprisonment for a term not
less than 1 year but not more than 3
years and a fine of not less than FRW
300,000 but not more than FRW
500,000.


(3) If assault or battery results in an illness
or non-permanent incapacity to work,
the penalty is imprisonment for a term
of not less than 3 years but not more
than 5 years and a fine of not less than
FRW 500,000 but not more than FRW
1,000,000.
(4) If assault or battery results in an
incurable illness, permanent total
incapacity to work, full loss of function
of an organ or mutilation for the victim,
the penalty is imprisonment for a term
of not less than 5 years but not more
than 10 years and a fine of not less than
FRW 1,000,000 but not more than
FRW 2,000,000.
(5) If assault or battery is committed with
premeditation or ambush, the offender
is liable to imprisonment for a term of
not less than 5 years but not more than
ten 10 years and a fine of not less than
FRW 2,000,000 but not more than FRW
3,000,000.
(6) If assault or battery results in death, the
penalty is imprisonment for a term of
not less than 10 years but not more than
20 years and a fine of not less than


FRW 3,000,000 but not more than
FRW 5,000,000.”
Article 12: Self-induced abortion or consent
to abortion
Article 123 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
« Article 123: Self-induced abortion or
consent to abortion
A person who self-induces an abortion or who,
contrary to this Law, consents to abortion
commits an offence. Upon conviction, he or
she is liable to imprisonment for a term of not
less than 1 year but not more than 3 years and
a fine of not less than FRW 100,000 but not
more than FRW 200,000. »
Article 13: Production, sale, distribution or
importation of products that are
substandard or harmful to human being
In Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018 determining
offences and penalties in general an Article
132 bis is inserted which is worded as follows:


« Article 132 bis: Production, sale,
distribution or importation products that
are substandard or harmful to human being
A person who produces, sells, distributes or
imports foods, beverages, medications or other
products which are substandard or which may
be harmful to the human being, commits an
offence. Upon conviction, he or she is liable to
imprisonment for a term of not less than 3 years
but not more than 5 years and a fine of not less
than FRW 1,000,000 but not more than FRW
3,000,000 FRW or only one of these
penalties.»
Article 14: Child defilement
Article 133 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
(1) « A person who commits one of the
following sexual acts on a child,
commits an offence:
(a) to insert a sexual organ of a person
into the sexual organ, anus or
mouth of a child;
(b) to insert any organ of the human


body into a sexual organ or anus of
a child;
(c) to perform any other act on the
body of a child for the purpose of
sexual arousal.
(2) A person convicted of committing any
of the acts referred to in Paragraph (1)
of this Article is liable to imprisonment
for a term of not less than 20 years but
not more than 25 years.
(3) If child defilement is committed on a
child under 14 years of age, the penalty
is life imprisonment.
(4) When child defilement is committed on
a child of 14 years of age or older and
caused him or her an incurable disease
or a disability, the penalty is life
imprisonment.
(5) When a person of full age commits
child defilement and subsequently
makes the child his or her husband or
wife, the penalty is increased to life
imprisonment.


(6) When a child who is at least 14 years of
age but less than 18 years of age
commits child defilement on a child
who is at least 14 years of age but less
than 18 years of age, through the use of
violence, threats, trickery or abuse of
the weakness of or administration of
psychoactive substances to the victim,
he or she is punished in accordance
with the provisions of Article 54 of this
Law.
(7) The offence of child defilement is
imprescriptible. »
Article 15: Bringing before judicial organs
false accusations against another person
Article 152 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
« A person who knowingly makes false
accusations against another person before
judicial organs, commits an offence. Upon
conviction, he or she is liable to imprisonment
for a term of not less than 1 year but not more
than 3 years and a fine of not less than FRW
500,000 but not more than FRW 1,000,000. If
false accusations are made in criminal case
where the accused is sentenced to


imprisonment for a term of more than 5 years,
the person having knowingly made such false
accusations against him or her is liable to
imprisonment for a term of not less than 3 years
but not more than 5 years and a fine of not less
than FRW 1,000,000 but not more than FRW
2,000,000. »
Article 16: Fraudulent retention of another
person’s found property
Article 172 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
« Article 172: Fraudulent retention of
another person’s found property
(1) A person who finds or accidentally
obtains a lost movable item belonging
to another person and fraudulently
retains it or gives it to another person,
commits an offence. Upon conviction,
he or she is liable to imprisonment for
a term of not less than 6 months but not
more than 2 years and a fine of not less
than FRW 50,000 but not more than
FRW 300,000 or only one of these
penalties.


(2) In case the accused person returns an
item found or obtained, the prosecution
of the offence is terminated at any stage
of the procedure. »
Article 17: Selling, pledging as a security a
property of another person or selling one’s
property given as security
Article 177 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
« Article 177: Selling, pledging as a security
a property of another person or selling one’s
property given as security
A person who sells, pledges as a security an
immovable or a movable property knowing
that he or she is not the owner or who sells his
or her property knowing that it is given as
security and does so without the prior consent
of the pledgee and the prospective purchaser
expressed in a notarial deed commits an
offence. Upon conviction, he or she is liable to
imprisonment for a term of not less than 1 year
but not more than 2 years and a fine of not less
than FRW 500,000 but not more than FRW
1,000,000. »


Article 18: Humiliation or insult against one
of the persons referred to under Article 217
Article 218 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is repealed.
Article 19: Obstructing or preventing the
enforcement of a final enforcement order
In Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018 determining
offences and penalties in general an Article 262
bis is inserted which is worded as follows:
« Article 262 bis: Obstructing or preventing
the enforcement of a final enforcement
order
A person who disposes of his or her property or
performs any other act to obstruct or prevent
the execution of a final enforcement order
commits an offence. Upon conviction, he or
she is liable to imprisonment for a term not less
than 6 months but not more than 2 years.


Article 20: Counterfeiting negotiable
instruments, use or circulation of
counterfeit negotiable instruments
Article 271 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:
« Article 271: Counterfeiting negotiable
instruments, use or circulation of
counterfeit negotiable instruments
Any person who counterfeits, knowingly uses
or circulates, by any means, counterfeit
negotiable instruments, commits an offence.
Upon conviction, he or she is liable to
imprisonment for a term of not less than 3 years
and not more than 5 years and a fine of 2 to 10
times the value of the negotiable instruments
counterfeited, used or circulated by him or
her.»
Article 21: Stripping off military ranks
Article 291 of Law nº 68/2018 of 30/08/2018
determining offences and penalties in general
is amended as follows:


« The penalty of stripping off military ranks is
imposed on a soldier if he or she –
(a) commits treason;
(b) surrenders to the enemy a post or a
position assigned to him or her or she
abandons other soldiers on the
battlefield;
(c) commits an act of insubordination or
revolt in wartime;
(d) refuses to go to his or her place of
deployment in wartime;
(e) deserts the army;
(f) commits the crime of genocide;
(g) commits the crime against humanity;
(h) commits the crime of rape;
(i) commits a child defilement;
(j) commits the offence of eating,
drinking, injecting himself or herself
with, inhaling, anointing himself or
herself with narcotic drugs and


psychotropic substances, or the offence
of unlawfully producing, transforming,
transporting, storing, giving to another
or selling narcotic drugs and
psychotropic substances; or
(k) is sentenced to a penalty of
imprisonment for a term of more than 5
years. »
Article 22: Language provision
This Law was drafted, considered and adopted
in Ikinyarwanda.
Article 23: Entry into force
This Law comes into force on the date of its
publication in the Official Gazette of the
Republic of Rwanda.
